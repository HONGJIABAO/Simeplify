# App Feature Requirements 

This document defines the core functionalities, business logic, and rules for the app. 

## 1. Onboarding & Profile
| ID | Feature | Description | Rules & Logic | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AUTH-01 | Login and Sign Up | Users create an account or log in before they start onboarding. | 1. Use Supabase Auth with email and password.<br>2. New users go to onboarding (ONB-01) after sign up.<br>3. Returning users go straight to the Simulator after login.<br>4. Password must be at least 8 characters.<br>5. Keep the user logged in, so they do not log in again each time.<br>6. Show clear error messages (wrong password, email already used).<br>7. Sign up also asks for the home address. It sets the tariff zone and the neighbourhood (COM-01). | High |
| ONB-01 | User Schedule | Users set their own schedule by entering the exact time ranges when they are not at home (busy, at work, or in class). | 1. No fixed options. The user picks a start time and an end time (e.g. 09:00 – 18:00).<br>2. The user can add more than one time range per day (e.g. 09:00 – 12:00 and 14:00 – 18:00).<br>3. The user can set weekdays and weekends separately, and copy one day to other days.<br>4. Time ranges on the same day cannot overlap. The end time must be later than the start time.<br>5. Any time not marked is treated as "at home".<br>6. The schedule is saved to Supabase and can be edited later.<br>7. This schedule is used by SIM-01 and SIM-02. | High |

Users also choose the current **star rating** (1 to 5 stars). If they do not know, they choose "Not sure" and the app treats the appliance as **3-star**.<br>3. The star rating is saved and used by SIM-02. | High |

A log out button ends the session and returns to Login.<br>6. Profile shows no row of summary numbers (money saved, total points, eco rating). That row was removed. | Medium |

## 2. Simulator
| ID | Feature | Description | Rules & Logic | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SIM-01 | Estimated Max Calculation | Automatically calculate the highest likely monthly electricity bill from appliance wattage, the user's schedule, and the user's day (SIM-07). | 1. Use data from ONB-01 (schedule), ONB-03 (appliances), and SIM-07 (My day).<br>2. Split each day into 30-minute slots. For each appliance, decide if it is on in each slot. Energy (kWh) = wattage × hours on ÷ 1000. Add all appliances, then convert to RM with the TNB tariff (see Appendix A).<br>3. **The calculation must factor in user presence.** Slots inside the "not at home" ranges from ONB-01 mean nobody is home. In those slots, appliances that need a person (air conditioner, lights, TV, fan, other) are **NOT** on, whatever My day says.<br>4. Appliances that run all day (fridge, router, standby devices) are on in all slots, even when nobody is home.<br>5. **Before the user answers any My day question:** every appliance that needs a person is on in all home slots. This gives the "max" bill.<br>6. **After My day is saved:** in a home slot, an appliance is on only if the block active in that slot lists it as on. In a slot with no block, only the living room lights are on, and only when dark (19:00 – 07:00).<br>7. Water heater (not slot based): kWh per day = showers per person per day × number of residents × minutes per shower ÷ 60 × wattage ÷ 1000. Default: 2 showers per person, 10 minutes each.<br>8. Weekdays and weekends use their own schedules. My day is the same on both. A month is 30 days (5/7 weekdays, 2/7 weekend days).<br>9. Recalculate right away when the schedule, appliances, My day, or a swap changes.<br>10. The estimate does not include the Energy Efficiency Incentive, taxes, or Time-of-Use rates. | High |
| SIM-02 | Smart Appliance Swap & Compare | When the user taps an old appliance, check its energy star rating, suggest a higher-rated replacement, and show the monthly saving. | 1. **Trigger rule:** Only trigger when the appliance is a "frequently used appliance" (high usage, based on the schedule in ONB-01). Do not suggest a swap for appliances that are rarely used.<br>2. **Dynamic star rating:** Read the star rating from ONB-03. Only suggest a **higher** star rating (e.g. if the original is 2-star, suggest 3-star, 4-star, or 5-star). If the original is already 5-star, show no suggestion. If the user chose "Not sure", the appliance counts as 3-star.<br>3. **Air conditioner rule:** If the appliance is an air conditioner, the suggestion must clearly say it is **Inverter** technology. The suggested air conditioner must be Inverter.<br>4. **Suggestion content:** The suggested star rating and the monthly saving (for air conditioners, also show Inverter). Example: "Replace with a 5-star Inverter air conditioner to save RM 80/month." Do not suggest a specific brand or model, and do not show a price. Users choose and buy by themselves.<br>5. Calculate the monthly saving with the same method as SIM-01 (including the hours when the user is home and not home).<br>6. After the user taps "Try swap", the estimated bill drops right away. This is a simulation only and is not saved. The user can undo it. | High |
| SIM-03 | Solar Panel Suggestion | Show a pop-up suggesting rooftop solar panels and the estimated saving (about 40%). | **Rule:** When the estimated monthly bill is RM 500 or more, the system opens the pop-up automatically. | Medium |
| SIM-04 | Savings Time Fast-Forward | Users tap one round button to open a time selector (1 month, 6 months, 1 year). Tapping the button a second time plays a fast-forward animation and shows the total saved after appliance swaps or solar installation. | 1. One round fast-forward button sits at the top right of the Simulator.<br>2. **First tap:** a selector opens with three stops: 1M, 6M, 1Y (default 1M). The user drags the slider or taps a stop. Nothing plays yet. The panel shows the total savings for the chosen period.<br>3. **Second tap:** the animation plays for the chosen period. Letting go of the slider does not play it.<br>4. Total savings = monthly saving × number of months (1, 6, or 12). The monthly saving comes from swaps and solar, calculated like SIM-01 (with presence and My day).<br>5. The animation: the calendar moves forward fast, the scene cycles through day and night, and the avatar (SIM-06) repeats a typical day (leaves, comes home, lights on, sleeps).<br>6. It takes about 4 s (1M), 6 s (6M), or 8 s (1Y). The savings number counts up with the calendar and ends at the total when the calendar reaches the end date.<br>7. The animation never changes the number. It only shows it.<br>8. Tapping the button or dragging the slider during playback stops the animation. The user taps again to play again.<br>9. The selector closes when the user taps outside it, or 10 s after the animation ends.<br>10. If the phone has "reduce motion" on, the second tap skips the animation and shows the final result.<br>11. With no swap and no solar, savings show RM 0. | High |
| SIM-05 | Interactive Floorplan | Users see their home as a 2.5D isometric floor plan and place appliances where they want. | 1. Build the floor plan from the rooms in ONB-02.<br>2. Use a hand-drawn 2.5D isometric view, drawn with 2D graphics. No real 3D engine.<br>3. Users can drag appliances to any position. The position is saved.<br>4. Tapping an appliance opens its panel (info, edit, and the swap suggestion from SIM-02).<br>5. Users can edit an appliance here. These edits change the real data. "Try swap" in SIM-02 is only a simulation. | High |
| SIM-06 | Resident Avatar | A small resident figure lives in the floor plan and follows the user's schedule and My day. | 1. One avatar shows the household's routine. It is drawn in the Natural Organic hand-drawn style (see design.md).<br>2. In the Simulator the avatar follows the phone clock and the schedule from ONB-01.<br>3. **Away time:** the avatar walks out of the door and the home shows an energy-saving look: lights off, and only always-on appliances (fridge, router) are active. A label shows when the user is back.<br>4. **Home time:** the avatar is in the room of the block active at that time (shower: bathroom, sleep: first bedroom, meal: kitchen, evening: living room, work or study: the room the user picked). With no active block, the avatar is in the living room.<br>5. The active block also decides which appliances are on (SIM-07). This is the same data that SIM-01 uses for the bill, so the picture and the bill always agree.<br>6. Before the user answers any My day question, the default blocks (see SIM-07) are used only to place the avatar. The bill still uses the max rule.<br>7. The avatar is only a picture of the data. It never changes the bill by itself. | Medium |
| SIM-07 | My Day (Quick Setup and Timeline) | Users tell the app what a normal day looks like. First with a few tap questions (Quick setup), then with a timeline they can adjust (My day). | 1. **First time:** when the user opens the Simulator after onboarding, the suggestion area asks "Answer a few quick questions?" with "Start" and "Later".<br>2. **Quick setup** has up to 4 cards. Everything is tap choices, no typing. Each card has Skip and Next. Cards for appliances the user does not have are skipped.<br>3. The cards: (1) sleep and wake time, (2) air conditioner while sleeping: no, all night, about 3 hours, about 5 hours (and bedroom fan on or off), (3) evening in the living room: time window, TV yes or no, air conditioner and fan on or off, (4) showers per person per day (only with a water heater).<br>4. Each question shows how the bill changes, for example "RM 260 → RM 235".<br>5. **Defaults** (used for skipped cards): sleep 23:00 – 07:00 with bedroom air conditioner and fan on and lights off; meals at 07:00, 12:00, and 19:00 for 30 minutes with kitchen lights on; evening 20:00 – 23:00 in the living room with lights, TV, air conditioner, and fan on; showers 2 per person per day, 10 minutes each.<br>6. **My day timeline:** a 24-hour list of blocks (sleep, meals, evening, showers, and blocks the user adds, such as work or study). Away blocks come from the schedule and are locked. The user taps a block to change its times and to switch appliances on or off. Only appliances in that room are listed.<br>7. Blocks cannot overlap. A block inside away time is ignored, and the app tells the user.<br>8. Until the user answers at least one question, the bill is the max bill and a note says so. Skipping everything keeps the max bill.<br>9. Answers are saved to Supabase. The bill, avatar, and savings update right away.<br>10. "Reset to default" brings back the default blocks. | High |

## 3. Scanner (Bill Scanning & Analysis)
| ID | Feature | Description | Rules & Logic | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SCN-01 | Bill Upload & Reading | Users can upload a photo or a PDF of their electricity and water bills. | Automatically find and read the usage and the amount on the bill. | High |
| SCN-02 | Anomaly Alert | Check each water bill for unusual usage and show a warning level. | 1. Applies to water bills. Two checks give a level (limits in Appendix B): the **change vs last month (MoM %)** and the **monthly volume (m³)**, scaled by the number of residents. The final level is the higher of the two.<br>2. Four levels: **Normal**, **Minor drip (watch)**, **Leak detected**, **Major pipe burst**.<br>3. Normal: no alert. Minor drip: one tip line in the result, no banner, no notification. Leak detected: amber "Anomaly detected" banner, a notification, and a tip to check the toilet cistern seal and outdoor pipes. Major pipe burst: the highest alert (terracotta), a notification, and advice to consider closing the main valve.<br>4. First water bill (no last month): use the volume check only.<br>5. The bill amount (RM) is shown but does not decide the level (see Appendix B).<br>6. The level is decided by these app rules. The AI only explains it (SCN-03).<br>7. Electricity bills get no anomaly level. They are compared with the Simulator estimate for energy points (COM-02). | High |
| SCN-03 | AI Analysis & Energy-Saving Tips | After a bill is saved, an AI analysis compares the bill with the user's bill history, explains it, and gives personal money-saving tips. | 1. Show an "AI Analysis" card on the result screen and on Bill Detail. Show a one-line summary on the Scanner home.<br>2. The AI reads: this bill, earlier bills of the same type (up to 6 months back, plus the same month last year if it exists), the Simulator estimate for that month (electricity), and the user's appliances, habits, and schedule. For water bills it uses household size and showers per day.<br>3. **History comparison.** The app calculates these numbers from the saved bills: change vs last month, difference vs the 3-month average, difference vs the same month last year, whether this bill is the highest or lowest of the last 6 months, and how many months in a row it went up or down. The AI explains what they mean. The AI does not calculate them.<br>4. Compare only with bills of **earlier** months than this bill. Numbers that need more history are hidden (for example, no 3-month average with only 2 bills).<br>5. The card has a short summary (2 sentences at most), the history comparison, up to 3 reasons, and 1 to 3 tips.<br>6. The warning (SCN-02) is decided by the app's rules. The AI only explains it. It never creates or removes a warning.<br>7. Every RM amount and percent in the text must come from numbers the app calculated. The AI must not make up numbers.<br>8. The analysis is saved with the bill, so opening an old bill does not call the AI again. The user can tap "Analyze again", for example after adding older bills.<br>9. The analysis loads after the bill is saved, so the result screen never waits for it. If it fails, show "Try again".<br>10. With only one bill (no history), compare with the Simulator estimate and say that a trend needs next month's bill. | Medium |
| SCN-04 | Bill History (Digital Archive) | Keep every saved bill so the user can look back and the AI can compare with it. | 1. Every saved bill is stored with its file (photo or PDF) and its values.<br>2. The Scanner home shows a monthly chart and a list of all bills, newest first. A filter switches between All, Electricity, and Water.<br>3. Tapping a bill opens Bill Detail: file, values, status, history comparison, and the saved AI analysis.<br>4. The user can delete a bill after a confirmation. Deleting a bill changes the history numbers of later analyses only when the user taps "Analyze again".<br>5. Only one bill per type per billing month. Saving the same month again replaces the old one after a confirmation. | Medium |

## Appendix A: TNB Domestic Tariff (for SIM-01)

Applies to Peninsular Malaysia (TNB), domestic users. This is the tariff structure that started on 1 July 2025. Checked on 17 October 2026 from news reports on the Energy Commission / TNB tariff. Check the TNB website before launch.

| Item | Rate | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Generation charge, up to 1,500 kWh/month | 27.03 sen/kWh | |
| Generation charge, above 1,500 kWh/month | 37.03 sen/kWh | Sources disagree if this rate applies to all kWh or only to the kWh above 1,500. Version 1 uses only the kWh above 1,500. Rare for homes. |
| Capacity charge | 4.55 sen/kWh | All kWh |
| Network charge | 12.85 sen/kWh | All kWh |
| Retail charge | RM 10 per month | Not charged when usage is 600 kWh or less |
| AFA (automatic fuel adjustment) | Changes every month | Can be positive or negative (for example -1.10 sen/kWh in September 2025). Store it in `tariff_rates` and update monthly. Default 0. |

**Formula (monthly bill in RM):**

```
generation = min(kWh, 1500) × 0.2703 + max(kWh - 1500, 0) × 0.3703
capacity   = kWh × 0.0455
network    = kWh × 0.1285
retail     = 10 if kWh > 600, else 0
afa        = kWh × afa_rate
bill       = generation + capacity + network + retail + afa
```

For a home under 1,500 kWh, the price is about 44.43 sen/kWh (plus AFA). A bill of RM 500 is about 1,100 kWh per month, so the solar suggestion (SIM-03) will only appear for heavy users.

**Not included in version 1:** the Energy Efficiency Incentive (a rebate for homes using 1,000 kWh or less), taxes, and the optional Time-of-Use tariff.

## Appendix B: Water Anomaly Levels (for SCN-02)

Used for water bills (Air Selangor and similar). The level is the **higher** of the MoM level and the volume level.

| Level | MoM change (vs last month) | Volume, 3 residents | What it usually means | What the app does |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Normal | below +20% (includes a fall) | up to 22 m³ | Hot weather, extra showers, car wash, weekend laundry | No alert |
| Minor drip (watch) | +20% to +29% | above 22 m³ and below 30 m³ | Loose tap or slow drip (1 drop per second is about 1.5 to 3 m³ per month) | A water-saving tip in the result. No banner, no notification. |
| Leak detected | +30% to +99% | 30 m³ or more | Ageing toilet cistern seal or silent outdoor pipe seepage (about 200 L per day, or +6 m³ per month) | Amber "Anomaly detected" banner, notification, and tip |
| Major pipe burst | +100% or more (doubled) | no volume rule | Underground pipe rupture or a stuck float valve (flowing 24 hours) | Terracotta top alert, notification, and advice to consider closing the main valve |

Rules:

- Round the MoM change to a whole percent before comparing (19.4% is Normal, 20% is Minor drip).
- **Volume limits scale with household size:** limit = value above × residents ÷ 3. Example for 4 residents: Normal up to 29 m³, Leak from 40 m³.
- Reference: Malaysians use about 200 to 230 L per person per day, about 6 to 7 m³ per person per month. A normal 3-person home uses about 18 to 21 m³.
- **The bill amount (RM) does not decide the level.** It comes from the volume, and the RM limits from the product notes do not match the m³ limits (RM 35 is about 33.6 m³, not 30 m³). The app still shows the RM amount.
- Water tariff used for that check: first 20 m³ at RM 0.65 per m³, 20 to 35 m³ at RM 1.62 per m³. This is from the product notes and is not checked yet. Check the Air Selangor website before launch.
- Example from the demo: 18 m³ and +48% vs September. Volume is Normal, MoM is Leak detected, so the level is **Leak detected**.
